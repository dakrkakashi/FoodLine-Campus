Vendor explicitly acknowledges that student payments flow 100% directly from student UPI apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) into the vendor’s designated commercial bank account:
• Pure Technology Gateway: FoodLine acts strictly as a Technical Service Provider (TSP) verifying transaction UTR hashes; FoodLine never holds, pools, or settles gross food funds.
• Zero Merchant Fees: 0.0% transaction commission deducted from cafeteria earnings; 100% instant liquidity for raw ingredient procurement.
Banking: T+0 direct settlement, zero float risk, 100% merchant cashflow autonomy

To eliminate the existential risk of being taxed on gross food turnover, FoodLine implements a legal dual-invoice architecture:
• Food Sales: Invoiced directly by the Canteen Vendor under their GSTIN (5% composite restaurant tax where applicable).
• Convenience Micro-Fee (3.5%): Invoiced exclusively by FoodLine Technologies Pvt Ltd (18% GST under SAC 998311 - Software Support Services).

• Routine verification of vendors’ annual water potability reports and medical fitness certificates under Schedule 4.
