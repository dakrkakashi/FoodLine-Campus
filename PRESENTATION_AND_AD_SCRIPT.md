# 🎬 FoodLine Campus — 40-Second Student App Video Advertisement Script & Storyboard

**Campaign Target:** 10,000+ Students at Sanjivani University, Kopargaon (and Maharashtra Campuses)  
**Primary Objective:** Drive immediate App Downloads & Pre-Orders for 15-Minute Recess / Break Time  
**Run Time:** Exactly 40 Seconds  
**Format:** 9:16 (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, WhatsApp Status) & 16:9 (Campus Kiosks, College Presentation Displays)
