## ⚡ Core Concepts & Domain Knowledge Encoded

### 1. Problem Statement: The Collegiate Break Bottleneck
- **40% Break Time Lost:** Average student spends **14.5 minutes in queues** for a 20-minute meal window.
- **Kitchen Rush Chaos:** Sudden 500-order demand surges without preparation forecast.
- **Payment Gateway Friction:** 2.0% + GST PG fees render micro-transactions (₹15–₹50) uneconomic for canteens.

### 2. The 4 Core Product Pillars
1. **Browse & Order Ahead:** Classroom pre-ordering on mobile web.
2. **Smart Slot Throttling:** 10-minute dynamic break slots balancing kitchen load.
3. **Option C Zero-Fee UPI:** Direct merchant standee scanning + 12-digit bank UTR verification (100% merchant payout).
4. **30-Second Express Handover:** Optical QR digital pass & 4-digit PIN verification at dedicated express counter.

### 3. Pilot Deployment Blueprint
- **Campus:** Sanjivani University, Kopargaon (MH)
- **Anchor Canteen:** Cafe @7
- **Break Slots:** 
  - Lunch Break (11:50 AM – 12:30 PM): Slot A (11:50–12:10), Slot B (12:10–12:30)
  - Evening Snack Break (2:30 PM – 2:50 PM)
- **Menu & Pricing:** Hot Samosa Pav (₹20), Butter Masala Dosa (₹50), Iced Cold Coffee (₹50), Cheese Grilled Sandwich (₹45), Veg Burger (₹50), Masala Chai (₹15), Nimbu Pani (₹25).

### 4. Market & Unit Economics
- **TAM (India):** 45,000+ Colleges · ₹18,000+ Cr ($2.2B)
- **SAM (Maharashtra):** 5,380 Colleges · 4.65M Students · ₹2,400 Cr ($290M)
- **SOM (Year 1-2):** 50 Campuses across MH clusters · ₹35 Cr GMV
- **Unit Economics:** CAC = ₹0 (organic QR adoption) · LTV = ₹2,400+/year · Gross Margin = 72% · Payback = 8 months.

- **UI:** shadcn/ui + Radix UI + OKLCH Dark Tokens + Nova Presets
- **Backend & Database:** Supabase PostgreSQL with RLS + Server-Sent Events (SSE) for zero-latency live tracking
