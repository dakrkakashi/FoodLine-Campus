# 💼 FoodLine Campus — Business Plan, Market Opportunity & Investor Deck
> **NotebookLM Knowledge Module 03** | **Domain:** Market Analysis, TAM/SAM/SOM, Pilot Traction, Unit Economics

## 1. Executive Summary
FoodLine Campus is scaling the critical B2B2C food ordering and payment infrastructure for Indian higher education institutions. By eliminating the 15-minute recess counter bottleneck, FoodLine unlocks trapped kitchen revenue, eliminates fake screenshot fraud, and captures 100% of student dining demand.

## 2. Market Sizing (India Higher Education)
- **Total Addressable Market (TAM): ₹35,000 Cr ($4.2 Billion)**
  - 43,000+ colleges and 1,100+ universities across India.
  - 4.1 crore (41 million) students spending an average of ₹40–₹80/day on meals and beverages over 220 academic days annually.
- **Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM): ₹8,400 Cr ($1.01 Billion)**
  - 10,000+ organized campuses (engineering, management, medical, autonomous colleges) with dedicated food concessions and high smartphone/UPI adoption.
- **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM): ₹420 Cr ($50 Million)**
  - 500 partner campuses across Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana, and Gujarat targeted over a 3-year expansion horizon.

## 3. Proven Pilot Traction (Sanjivani University, Kopargaon)
Live operations at Sanjivani University across 5 campus food outlets (flagship: **Cafe @7**):
- **Meals Delivered:** 544+ verified hot meals prepared and collected.
- **Gross Merchandise Value (GMV):** ₹35,360+ processed seamlessly.
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** ₹65.00 per student order combo.
- **Repeat Retention Rate:** 82% repeat order rate among registered students within 30 days.
- **Express Pickup Time:** <45 seconds average pickup time from arrival to handover.
- **Overbooking Rate:** 0.0% overbooking across all pilot slots (enforced by 60-slot governor).
- **Fraud Losses:** Zero verified UPI fraud losses on completed orders.

## 4. Business Model & Unit Economics
- **Student Guarantee:** 100% free. No app fee, no convenience fee, exact counter menu prices.
- **Revenue Model:** 3.5% performance commission per completed order (~₹2.275 net on ₹65 AOV).
- **Direct-to-Vendor UPI:** UPI payments route directly to vendor bank VPAs. Zero capital custody, eliminating RBI payment aggregator licensing friction.
- **Break-Even Threshold:** A canteen partner breaks even on software adoption at just **659 orders/month**, while typical university canteen volumes reach 1,500–2,000 orders/day during semester runs.
