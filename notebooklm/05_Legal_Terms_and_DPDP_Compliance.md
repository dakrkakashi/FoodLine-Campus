# 📜 FoodLine Campus — Statutory Legal Terms & DPDP Compliance
> **NotebookLM Knowledge Module 05** | **Domain:** Legal, FSSAI, DPDP Act 2023, SLAs, Grievance Framework

## 1. Master Terms Overview
The platform operates under a comprehensive 25-section legal agreement (`FoodLine_Campus_Terms_Source.md`) establishing clear operational and statutory boundaries:
- **Entity & Technical Intermediary Role:** FoodLine Campus functions as a technology platform connecting students with independently licensed campus cafeterias. Food preparation, hygiene, raw material handling, and counter handover are the direct statutory obligations of the Cafeteria Partner.
- **FSSAI Food Safety Compliance:** Partner canteens must hold active FSSAI licenses (e.g., Cafe @7 FSSAI Lic #11522036000142). Ingredients and common allergens (gluten, nuts, dairy) are prominently displayed on digital menus.
- **20-Minute Thermal Holding SLA:** Canteens maintain prepared food at >65°C in temperature-controlled holding units for up to 20 minutes past the scheduled slot time before disposal.
- **Stockout & Refund Protection:** If an item sells out after payment confirmation, the kitchen system triggers a 100% direct refund to the student's UPI VPA within 2 hours.

## 2. Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 Compliance
- **Data Minimization:** Student accounts collect only necessary identifiers: PRN (student ID), full name, mobile number, and department.
- **Privacy by Design & Phone Masking:** Kitchen KDS displays and public announcer monitors mask student contact numbers (`+91 ******1234`) to protect privacy from third parties.
- **Zero Third-Party Telemetry Monetization:** Strict statutory commitment prohibiting the sale or sharing of student dietary, demographic, or financial telemetry.
- **Right to Erasure & Grievance Hierarchy:** Students may request account closure and record erasure. Formal inquiries are processed through a 3-tier escalation framework:
  1. Level 1: On-campus Canteen Representative (turnaround: 15 minutes during break).
  2. Level 2: Digital Support Desk (`support@foodlinecampus.in`, turnaround: 4 hours).
  3. Level 3: Statutory Grievance Officer (`grievance@foodlinecampus.in`, statutory turnaround: 48 hours).
