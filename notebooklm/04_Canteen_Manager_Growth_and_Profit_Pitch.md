# 📈 FoodLine Campus — Canteen Manager Growth & Profit Pitch
> **NotebookLM Knowledge Module 04** | **Domain:** Canteen ROI, Operational Workflow, Revenue Multiplication

## 1. The Canteen Operator's Reality
During short 15-to-20-minute lecture breaks, a single physical counter can process only 12–15 customers per minute. Even with multiple staff, total counter throughput tops out at **150–180 meals per break**, leaving 300+ students unserved.

Meanwhile, chaotic counter rush causes:
- **₹3,000 to ₹6,000 daily losses** from students flashing fake UPI screenshots.
- **18% to 22% food wastage** due to blind prep without pre-order forecasting.
- **Lost sales** as students leave empty-handed to avoid being late to class.

## 2. The 4 Operational Transformations with FoodLine
1. **3x Break Volume Capacity:** Canteens batch-cook and bag 500+ meals *before* the bell rings. When the break begins, orders are handed over in <30 seconds via express racks.
2. **100% Payment Security:** Every order requires a verified 12-digit bank UTR checked against duplicate replay attacks. Zero unpaid food handovers.
3. **80% Less Food Waste:** Accurate pre-order reports tell chefs exactly how many dosas, samosas, and sandwiches to prep, slashing scrap rates to <4%.
4. **Bigger Baskets (Combo Upsells):** High-resolution digital menus encourage pairing snacks with cold coffee or chai, lifting ticket sizes from ₹45 to ₹65 (+44%).

## 4. Hardware Requirements
- **Kitchen Tablet / Smartphone:** Runs the web-based Kitchen Display System (`/kds`). No special POS terminal required.
- **Express Rack:** A simple numbered shelving rack (Counters 1–4) for bagged pre-orders.
- **Counter Cashier Phone:** Used to verify 4-digit pickup OTPs (`/api/orders/verify-otp`) during pickup.
