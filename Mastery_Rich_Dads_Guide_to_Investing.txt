PART 1: THE CORE PHILOSOPHY & THE 90/10 RULE OF MONEY
================================================================================

1. THE 90/10 RULE OF MONEY:
   - In the game of golf, 10% of the players make 90% of the money.
   - In investing, 10% of the investors make 90% of all the profits.
   - The poor and middle class invest as "outsiders" through mutual funds and 401(k)s.
   - The rich invest as "insiders" and "creators of assets".

2. INVESTING IS A PLAN, NOT A PRODUCT:
   - Most people think investing is buying stocks, mutual funds, or real estate.
   - Kiyosaki teaches that investing is NOT a product or technique; 
     investing is a personal, step-by-step SYSTEMATIC PLAN.
   - A good investment plan should be simple, mechanical, and even boring.

3. THE THREE LIFE CHOICES:
   Every person must prioritize these 3 choices in order:
   A. Choice 1: To be Secure (Safe job, savings, pension)
   B. Choice 2: To be Comfortable (Nice house, decent cars, yearly vacation)
   C. Choice 3: To be Rich (Financial freedom, passive cash flow, generational assets)
   * The poor and middle class choose Security first, then Comfort.
   * The wealthy choose TO BE RICH FIRST, then Comfort, and Security is guaranteed.

PART 2: THE 3 TYPES OF INCOME & EDUCATION
================================================================================

THE 3 TYPES OF EDUCATION:
1. Academic Education (Reading, writing, math)
2. Professional Education (Becoming a doctor, engineer, accountant, plumber)
3. Financial Education (Understanding assets vs. liabilities, cash flow, taxes, and debt)

THE 3 TYPES OF INCOME:
1. Earned Income:
   - Money you work for (salary, hourly wage).
   - Highest taxed income (up to 35-50%). Hardest to build wealth with.
2. Portfolio Income:
   - Capital gains from buying low and selling high (stocks, flips).
   - Subject to market volatility and capital gains taxes.
3. Passive Income:
   - Cash flow from businesses, real estate rentals, royalties, dividends.
   - Lowest taxed income (often near 0% legally with depreciation & deductions).
   - THE SECRET OF THE RICH: Continuously convert Earned Income into Passive Income!

PART 3: THE 5 LEVELS OF INVESTORS
================================================================================

LEVEL 1: THE ACCREDITED INVESTOR
- Earns high income ($200k+ / year) or high net worth ($1M+).
- Still buys retail securities, but qualifies for private placements.

LEVEL 2: THE QUALIFIED INVESTOR
- Understands fundamental analysis (financial statements) and technical analysis (market charts).
- Knows how to evaluate stocks, options, and market cycles from the outside.

LEVEL 3: THE SOPHISTICATED INVESTOR
- Understands the 3 E's: Education, Experience, and Excessive Cash.
- Understands the tax code, corporate entities (LLCs, C-Corps), and legal protections.
- Masters the 10 Investor Controls.

LEVEL 4: THE INSIDE INVESTOR
- Owns a significant stake or manages the business/real estate.
- Has direct control over management, operations, revenues, and strategy.

LEVEL 5: THE ULTIMATE INVESTOR (THE CREATOR)
- The highest level: Builds a successful business (B-Quadrant) and takes it public (IPO) 
  or sells it for massive equity (e.g., Elon Musk, Jeff Bezos, Warren Buffett).
- Creates the shares that Level 1, 2, and retail investors buy!

PART 4: THE SACRED B-I TRIANGLE (HOW TO BUILD ASSETS)
================================================================================

The B-I Triangle represents the architecture of a multi-million-dollar business.

         / \
        / P \          P = Product (Top / Smallest Level)
       /-----\
      /   L   \        L = Legal
     /---------\
    /     S     \      S = Systems
   /-------------\
  /       C       \    C = Communications (Sales & Marketing)
 /-----------------\
/         CF        \  CF = Cash Flow Management (Base / Most Important)
---------------------
  MISSION  |  TEAM  |  LEADERSHIP  (The 3 Pillars of the Outer Frame)

1. THE OUTER FRAME (The Foundation):
   - MISSION: A spiritual and commercial purpose that solves a deep human problem.
   - TEAM: You cannot be the smartest person in the room. Surround yourself with 
     top accountants, attorneys, and operators.
   - LEADERSHIP: Inspiring others, making hard decisions, and taking full responsibility.

2. THE INNER 5 LAYERS:
   - 1. CASH FLOW: Managing inflow vs. outflow, daily runway, burn rate, and margins.
   - 2. COMMUNICATIONS: Marketing, copywriting, branding, negotiation, and public relations.
   - 3. SYSTEMS: Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), supply chain, automation, software.
   - 4. LEGAL: Intellectual property, contracts, shareholder agreements, tax structures.
   - 5. PRODUCT: The physical item or service. (The least important part of the triangle! 
        McDonald's doesn't make the world's best burger, but it has the world's best system!)

PART 5: THE 10 INVESTOR CONTROLS OF THE RICH
================================================================================

1. Control Over YOURSELF:
   - Emotional control. Fear and greed destroy average investors.
   - Financial literacy (reading balance sheets and P&L statements).

2. Control Over INCOME / EXPENSE RATIOS:
   - Keep personal living expenses low while maximizing business income conversion.

3. Control Over TAX MANAGEMENT:
   - Using corporations, real estate depreciation, and tax codes to minimize taxes legally.

4. Control Over WHEN YOU BUY AND SELL:
   - Buying when there is blood in the streets (recessions) and holding for cash flow.

5. Control Over TRANSACTION COSTS:
   - Minimizing broker fees, commissions, and slippage.

6. Control Over the E-T-C (Entity, Timing, Character of Income):
   - Choosing the right legal entity (LLC vs C-Corp) and transforming Earned -> Passive income.

7. Control Over TERMS AND CONDITIONS:
   - Writing the deal contract to protect your downside and guarantee cash flow.

8. Control Over ACCESS TO INFORMATION:
   - Knowing the market metrics, cap rates, and trends before the general public.

9. Control Over GIVING BACK (Philanthropy):
   - Generosity, charity, and creating jobs/housing for the community.

10. Control Over the EXIT STRATEGY:
    - Knowing how to refinance, sell, 1031-exchange, or pass assets to heirs tax-free.

PART 6: THE MASTER WEALTH FORMULA
================================================================================

1. Step 1: Build or master a business in the B-Quadrant (or a high-income skill).
2. Step 2: Extract excess cash flow from the business.
3. Step 3: Funnel that cash flow into real estate and cash-flowing assets (I-Quadrant).
4. Step 4: Use tax depreciation and debt leverage to compound wealth exponentially.
5. Step 5: Repeat until your passive cash flow exceeds your living expenses by 10x!
