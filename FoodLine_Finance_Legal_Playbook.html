Entity Structure — LLP

Register a Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) before launch. It's the cheapest structure that gives you limited liability, a business bank account, and a clean 50-50 ownership split.

Verdict

Register an LLP now. It costs ₹5,000–10,000, protects your personal assets, and converts to a Pvt Ltd later when you raise venture money.

Factor Informal (No Entity) LLP (Recommended) Pvt Ltd
Registration cost ₹0 ₹5,000–10,000 ₹15,000–25,000
Annual compliance ₹0 ₹10,000–15,000 ₹50,000–1,00,000
Separate bank account No (personal) Yes Yes
Limited liability No Yes Yes
Investor credibility Zero Moderate High
GST registration Per individual On the LLP On the company
Tax rate 30% personal slab 30% on LLP profits 25% (< ₹400Cr turnover)

Why LLP, not Pvt Ltd

  • You're pre-revenue, pre-launch — don't pay for corporate machinery you don't need yet
  • Pvt Ltd needs board meetings, annual returns & mandatory audit even at ₹0 revenue
  • Convert LLP → Pvt Ltd later under Section 366, Companies Act
  • Clean 50-50 structure + limited liability from day one

Registration checklist

  • Both partners' PAN cards
  • Address proof (both partners)
  • Name reservation: "FoodLine Campus LLP"
  • LLP Agreement (drafted on stamp paper)
  • Form FiLLiP on the MCA Portal
Timeline & Cost Approval in 7–15 days. Total cost ₹5,000–10,000 including stamp paper and professional fees. Do this the same week you read this.

GST on Your 4% Platform Commission

You don't need GST registration yet. When you cross ₹20 lakh commission turnover, you charge 18% GST on the platform fee only — never on the food.

Verdict

No GST registration needed now. You're ₹4/order × ~16 years away from the ₹20L threshold. Register only on your commission income level (not on food).

Component Who charges GST Rate When
Food (₹100 item) Canteen owner 5% Already on canteen's bill
Platform fee (₹4) You (the LLP) 18% Only when annual commission > ₹20L

The math, full walkthrough

Student pays = ₹104.00 # ₹100 food + ₹4 platform fee Your commission = ₹4.00 GST on commission = ₹0.72 # 18% of ₹4 Your net commission = ₹3.28 // BELOW ₹20L turnover — current state Commission turnover = < ₹20,00,000/yr → No GST registration needed → You pocket ₹4.00 per order → Canteen handles food GST alone // ABOVE ₹20L turnover — future state → Register LLP for GST → Charge 18% GST on ₹4 commission → File GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B monthly → You keep ₹3.28 per order

When do you cross ₹20L?

At ₹100 AOV × 4% commission = ₹4/order Threshold = ₹20,00,000 Orders needed = 20,00,000 ÷ 4 = 5,00,000 At 100 orders/day × 300 days = 30,000 orders/yr Years to GST threshold = 5,00,000 ÷ 30,000 = ~16.7 years

SAC Code for your fee

  • SAC 998314 — Other IT services n.e.c. (online platform services)
  • Same category Swiggy & Zomato use for commission income
  • Apply this when you register — not now

Are you a Section 9(5) e-commerce operator?

  • No — payment flows straight to the canteen's UPI, you never custody food money
  • Section 9(5) CGST applies only to operators who collect payment on suppliers' behalf
  • ⚠ If you switch to collecting ₹104 first, you become a 9(5) operator and must register immediately regardless of turnover

Razorpay charges 0% for UPI — and ~95% of your campus payments will be UPI. Net gateway cost at pilot scale: under ₹400/month.

Verdict

Use Razorpay. UPI = 0% fee. Add a 2% convenience fee on card payments so everyone's pushed to UPI. Your gateway cost stays near zero.

Your campus cost projection

100 orders/day @ ₹104 AOV: UPI orders = 95 × ₹0 = ₹0.00 Card orders = 5 × ₹2.08 = ₹10.40/day Monthly gateway cost = ₹10.40 × 30 = ₹312/month # Effectively free — automation replaces 2–3 hrs/day manual UTR work

Pricing strategy — push everyone to UPI

  • UPI payment: ₹104.00 (no extra charge)
  • Card payment: ₹106.04 (+2% convenience fee)
  • Students already live on UPI — this costs you almost nothing

Direct UPI collection (no gateway)

  • Free, zero fees — what you do today
  • Manual UTR reconciliation = full-time job at 500+ orders
  • Fine for pilot scale (50/day), fatal at scale
  • Use Razorpay once live volume passes ~100 orders/day

50-50 Partnership — You & Kanakshree

The LLP agreement makes the split legally enforceable. GST is on the LLP, profits flow 50:50 to each partner, and each partner pays income tax at their own slab.

Recommendation

Localize the 50-50 split in the LLP agreement today. Verbal partnership agreements are India's #1 source of startup disputes.

With an LLP (recommended)

FoodLine Campus LLP collects 4% commission LLP charges 18% GST on commission # only > ₹20L turnover LLP net income → distributed 50:50 Each partner gets: ₹2.00 / order (before personal income tax) // Personal income tax — New Regime (FY 2026-27) Annual income Tax < ₹3,00,000 0% ₹3–7L 5% ₹7–10L 10% ₹10–12L 15% ₹12–15L 20% > ₹15L 30%

Without an LLP (current state)

2% commission directly to each person Each partner income @100 orders/day: ₹2 × 100 × 300 days = ₹60,000/year → No GST needed (below ₹20L each) → BUT: no limited liability → BUT: no business bank account → BUT: no legal dispute framework → BUT: zero investor credibility
The one document you must not skip The LLP Agreement captures ownership, profit split, roles, exit rules, and what happens on a dispute. Both partners sign on stamp paper. This is the document thao protects your ₹2/order and her ₹2/order — don't rely on friendship.

Razorpay Setup — Step by Step

Register, integrate, and go live. 2–3 days for KYC, 1–2 days for integration.

  1. Step 1 · Register Create a Razorpay account in the LLP business name (you need the LLP registered first). ₹0
  2. Step 2 · KYC Submission Submit LLP PAN, Partnership Deed, business bank account, Aadhaar + PAN of authorized signatory, address proof. ₹0
  3. Step 3 · Integration Integrate Razorpay Checkout via JS SDK or API. Create an order for ₹104 (₹100 + 4% platform fee), student pays through checkout. Developer time
  4. Step 4 · Webhooks & Automation Razorpay sends a confirmation webhook → your backend auto-confirms the order. Kills the manual UTR reconciliation workflow. ₹0
  5. Step 5 · Go Live UPI = free, cards = 2% convenience fee passes to the student. Launch at Cafe @7. ₹0
Why Razorpay beats your current UTR model Instant confirmation, zero manual entry, no fake-UTR risk, automatic reconciliation. At 500 orders/day this automation saves you the equivalent of a part-time salary.

Risks & Caveats

Five things that can break this plan — and how each one is handled.

1 · LLP → Pvt Ltd conversion fees

If you raise real VC money, conversion costs ₹50,000–1,00,000 and takes 3–6 months. Not a problem — just plan it when the round is real.

2 · GST at scale

18% GST on commission is unavoidable past ₹20L turnover. Budget it into your financial model from day one so margins aren't a surprise.

3 · UTR verification doesn't scale

Works at pilot, breaks at 500+ orders/day. Razorpay is the exit ramp — implement before volume grows, not after.

4 · No canteen agreement = existential risk

Without a signed agreement, the canteen owner can walk away with your students any day. The Canteen Partner Agreement is your #1 contract priority after incorporation.

5 · Verbal partnership split

Friendship-founded 50-50 splits are the most common startup litigation in India. The LLP Agreement removes ambiguity: ownership, roles, exit, dispute resolution — in writing.
