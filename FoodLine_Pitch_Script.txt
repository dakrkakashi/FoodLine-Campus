SLIDE 5: PAYMENT INNOVATION (OPTION C)
================================================================================
📌 SLIDE TITLE: Option C: Zero-Fee UPI & UTR Verification
🎯 9-YEAR-OLD MEMORY HOOK: "No credit card taxes! 100% of your ₹20 goes directly to the uncle making your samosa."

🗣️ WHAT TO SAY ALOUD:
"Why do traditional payment gateways fail on campus? 
Because if a student buys a ₹15 cup of tea, standard gateways take 2% plus taxes. 
Canteen owners can't afford that!
So we designed 'Option C':
1. The student sees the official Cafe @7 UPI QR code in the app.
2. They pay the exact rupee amount directly using GPay, PhonePe, or Paytm.
3. They enter the 12-digit bank UTR receipt number.
4. Our system instantly verifies it and gives them a VIP digital token.
Zero transaction fees, zero gateway commissions, and 100% of the money lands in 
the canteen owner’s bank instantly!"

SLIDE 6: KITCHEN DISPLAY SYSTEM (KDS) & REAL-TIME SYNC
================================================================================
📌 SLIDE TITLE: Kitchen Display System & Real-Time Sync
🎯 9-YEAR-OLD MEMORY HOOK: "The cooks get a video-game screen telling them: 'Cook 15 Dosas for 11:50 AM!' No paper, no confusion!"

🗣️ WHAT TO SAY ALOUD:
"Behind the counter, we equip the cooks with a smart tablet dashboard:
1. Smart Slot Batching: The screen groups orders automatically. It tells the cook: 
   '15 dosas needed for the 11:50 AM slot.' The chef cooks in batches instead of panic!
2. Server-Sent Events (SSE): When the chef marks an order 'READY', the student's phone 
   screen dings in real-time with zero refreshing.
3. Optical QR Handover: The staff member scans the student's phone QR code with 
   a camera, hands over the food, and the order is marked done in 2 seconds.
4. 1-Tap Out of Stock: If cold coffee runs out, one tap disables it so nobody orders 
   something that isn't available."
