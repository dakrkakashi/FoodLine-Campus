Payment Innovation

Option C: Zero-Fee UPI & UTR Verification

Solving the micro-transaction dilemma: 0% payment gateway fees on ₹20 campus items.

Step 1 📱

Scan Standee QR

Student views Cafe @7 UPI QR Standee image and UPI ID displayed in the FoodLine app.

Step 2 ⚡

Pay via Any UPI App

Pays exact total directly via GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, or CRED — with zero platform fee.

Step 3 🔑

Submit 12-Digit UTR

Inputs bank UTR number (or last 6 digits) into FoodLine verification modal for validation.

Step 4 🎟️

Generate Token & Pass

Backend validates uniqueness, prevents replay, and issues verified digital token #FL-XXXX.

🏆 WHY OPTION C WINS ON CAMPUS

Traditional Razorpay/Paytm PG charges 2.0% + GST per transaction — unfeasible for low ticket ₹20–50 canteen items. Option C ensures 100% merchant payout directly to the canteen's bank account with zero intermediary commissions.

Kitchen Intelligence

Live Kitchen Display System (KDS) & SSE

Empowering cafeteria cooks with automated batch forecasting and real-time handovers.

📈

Live Slot Batching

Groups incoming orders by upcoming 10-minute break slots so kitchen staff can batch-cook (e.g., 15 Dosas for 11:50 AM). Reduces waste and prep chaos.

📡

Server-Sent Events (SSE)

Real-time bi-directional pipeline updates order status from PREPARING → READY with zero screen refreshes. Students see live progress.

📷

Optical QR Handover

Express counter staff scan student digital QR code or enter 4-digit PIN for sub-1-minute meal handovers. No paper tokens needed.

📦

Live Inventory Toggle

1-tap out-of-stock switcher prevents students from ordering sold-out dishes during sudden surges. Real-time menu sync across all devices.
